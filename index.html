About the Event

The Clan Clash Cup is a fan-run Clash of Clans tournament where clans battle in head-to-head wars to become champion. Teams of 5 players attack each other's bases, and the clan that earns the most stars moves on to the next round. The event is open to all players at Town Hall 18, from casual clans to experienced war veterans.

This video demonstrates how esports is played in esports mode.

Every match is livestreamed, and the top 3 clans win prizes!
